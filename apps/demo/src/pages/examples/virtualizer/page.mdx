## How it works

`EmojiPicker.List` virtualizes with React Aria. To use another virtualizer, call `useEmojiGrid({ rowHeight, headerHeight, columns, scrollToIndex })` inside `EmojiPicker.Root` in place of `List`. It returns `rows`, each a section title or a row of emoji with its `size`, `getGridProps`, `getHeaderProps`, and `variations`, the skin-tone popup to render in the scroll container. `useEmojiCell(grid.state, item)` returns a cell's `cellProps`. Keyboard, highlight, hover, long press, and skin-tone variations work as in `List`. Pass `scrollToIndex` so the arrow keys scroll your virtualizer.

A bundle that does not use `List` leaves out React Aria's `Virtualizer` when the bundler drops unused properties of the `EmojiPicker` namespace, as Vite does. With esbuild, import flat exports such as `EmojiPickerRoot` instead.

## react-window and Virtuoso

The same `rows` feed react-window's `rowComponent` and Virtuoso's `itemContent`. Render `grid.rows[index]` with the same props, and call their `scrollToRow` or `scrollToIndex` from `scrollToIndex`.
